NEET-style questions across Physics, Chemistry & Biology

PHYSICS
A particle moves in a circle of radius R. What is the displacement after half a revolution?
▼
A) 0
B) R
C) 2R ✓
D) πR
EXPLANATION
After half a revolution, the particle moves from one end of the diameter to the other. The displacement is the straight-line distance between start and end points, which equals the diameter = 2R. Note: distance travelled = πR (semicircle), but displacement = 2R.
CHEMISTRY
Which element has the highest electronegativity?
▼
A) Oxygen
B) Nitrogen
C) Fluorine ✓
D) Chlorine
EXPLANATION
Fluorine (F) has the highest electronegativity of all elements — 3.98 on the Pauling scale. Electronegativity generally increases across a period and up a group. Fluorine is at the top-right of the periodic table (excluding noble gases), making it the most electronegative.
BIOLOGY
The powerhouse of the cell is the ___
▼
A) Nucleus
B) Ribosome
C) Mitochondria ✓
D) Golgi body
EXPLANATION
Mitochondria produce ATP (adenosine triphosphate) — the cell's primary energy currency — through aerobic respiration via the Krebs cycle and oxidative phosphorylation. They have their own DNA and double membrane structure, consistent with the endosymbiotic theory.
PHYSICS
Newton's second law relates force to ___
▼
A) Velocity
B) Acceleration ✓
C) Displacement
D) Momentum
EXPLANATION
Newton's Second Law states: F = ma (Force = mass × acceleration). The net force acting on an object equals the product of its mass and the acceleration produced. Note: while force is the rate of change of momentum (F = dp/dt), the law directly relates force to acceleration for constant mass.
BIOLOGY
DNA replication is ___
▼
A) Conservative
B) Semi-conservative ✓
C) Dispersive
D) None of the above
EXPLANATION
DNA replication is semi-conservative — each new DNA molecule contains one original (parental) strand and one newly synthesised strand. This was experimentally proved by Meselson and Stahl (1958) using ¹⁵N/¹⁴N labelling in E. coli, a landmark experiment often asked in NEET.
